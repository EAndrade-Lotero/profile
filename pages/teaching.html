--- layout: page title: Teaching permalink: /teaching/ --- At the heart of my teaching philosophy is the belief that students learn complex ideas more deeply when they can examine how those ideas are constructed from simpler ones and test them in concrete cases. Since 2002, I have taught students in psychology, philosophy, mathematics, economics, business, and computer science. I have taught courses ranging from logic and philosophy of language to linear algebra, theory of computation, artificial intelligence, and reinforcement learning. Across these disciplines and student populations, I design hands-on, interactive activities that make complex processes visible and give students an active role in developing their understanding. **[Courses]({{ site.baseurl }}/teaching/courses/)** Recent syllabi from the Master's in Computer Science (MACC): - [Artificial intelligence]({{ site.baseurl }}/teaching/courses/#artificial-intelligence) - [Reinforcement learning]({{ site.baseurl }}/teaching/courses/#reinforcement-learning)
