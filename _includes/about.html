I am an Associate Professor at the School of Sciences and Engineering at Universidad del Rosario (Bogotá, Colombia) and a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Communication at the University of California, Davis.

My research examines how people develop representations that organize complementary roles in groups. With Robert Goldstone, I have combined group experiments and computational models to study the division of cognitive and linguistic labor. At UC Davis, working with Seth Frey and in collaboration with Nori Jacoby, Dalton Conley, and Ofer Tchernichovski, I am extending this program to role-based power, asking how control over the rules of interaction affects collective outcomes.
